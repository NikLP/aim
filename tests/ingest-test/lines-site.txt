The library runs a home delivery service for housebound members, with deliveries every Wednesday.
The library holds a rare maps collection in the local history room, viewable by appointment.
Toddler Storytime takes place every Friday at 11am in the Children's Room.
The ground-floor cafe is run by Dockside Coffee and is open from 9am to 4pm on weekdays.
The adult book club meets on the first Monday of every month at 7pm in the reading room.
Inter-library loans cost 3 pounds per item and usually arrive within 10 days.
Members can read digital newspapers and magazines for free through PressReader with their library card.
A family history help desk runs every Wednesday from 2pm to 4pm.
There are four study pods on the first floor, each bookable for up to 2 hours.
The car park has 20 spaces, parking is free for 2 hours, and there are two blue badge bays by the entrance.
Lost property is kept at the main desk for 4 weeks and then given to charity.
Scanning to email is free at the public computers.
