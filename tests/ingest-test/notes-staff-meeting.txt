Staff meeting, Harbourside Community Library, Tuesday morning. Present: Alex, the two branch assistants and the Children's Librarian.

Alex opened by saying the home delivery service has been a success so far and will carry on every Wednesday. Someone asked about the cafe: Dockside Coffee will keep running it and it stays open 9am to 4pm on weekdays. Nobody was sure about parking, so Alex checked and confirmed there are 20 spaces, free for two hours, with two blue badge bays by the entrance.

On events: the Language Cafe is still on Thursdays at 6pm in the meeting room, and Alex wants to add drop-in tech help on Mondays at 10am. The Children's Librarian said Toddler Storytime stays on Fridays at 11am. We agreed that lost property will be kept for four weeks and then given to charity. Oh, and somebody left a umbrella in the reading room again, which is not really a fact worth keeping.

Finally, the book sale: the trustees want it moved to the second weekend of June from next year.
